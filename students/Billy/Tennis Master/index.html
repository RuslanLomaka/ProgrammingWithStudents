The Scoring Hierarchy

  • Point Progression: Points go from 0 (Love) → 15 → 30 → 40 → Game.
  • Deuce & Advantage: If score reaches 40-40, it is called Deuce. A player must win two consecutive points from Deuce to win the game (Advantage → Game).
  • Sets: A player wins a set by reaching 6 games first, with a margin of at least 2 games (e.g., 6-4). If tied at 6-6, a 7-point Tiebreak is played.
  • Matches: Matches are typically played as Best-of-3 sets (or Best-of-5 in Men's Grand Slam events).
2

Serving Rules & Rotation

  • Service Diagonal: The server always begins each game from behind the right (Deuce) court baseline, serving diagonally into the opponent's right service box.
  • Two Attempts: Serves get two chances (1st serve & 2nd serve). Missing both results in a Double Fault and point loss.
  • Foot Fault: The server's feet must NOT touch the baseline or inside the court before striking the ball.
  • Let Serve: If a serve clips the net cord but lands inside the correct service box, a Let is called, and the server re-takes that serve.
3

In vs. Out Lines

  • Lines are IN: If any part of the ball touches any portion of the white boundary line (even 1 millimeter!), the ball is legally IN.
  • One Bounce Limit: The ball can only bounce once on your side of the court before you return it over the net.
  • Net Touches: Players or their racquets cannot touch the net at any point during an active point, or they lose the point immediately.
4

Changeovers & Tiebreaks

  • Side Changeovers: Players swap sides of the court on every odd game total in a set (e.g., at 1-0, 2-1, 3-2, 4-3, 5-0).
  • 7-Point Tiebreak: At 6-6 in a set, a tiebreak is played. First to reach 7 points with a 2-point lead wins the set (e.g. 7-5, 8-6).
  • Tiebreak Serve Rotation: Server A serves 1 point. Then Server B serves 2 points, Server A serves 2 points, alternating until finished.
